## Why this file exists

This source object was isolated only after the repository/commit audit. A repository search for `Ösztön és nevelés` returned no prior research file, so this is not a repetition of the earlier Hungarian sweeps.

The source is important because it is a **1939 publication by Szondi himself** that reports experimental drive-diagnostic work on twins, i.e. exactly the period in which repeated administration was already operational and immediately adjacent to the reported 1939–1940 development window of Böszörményi's Inkonstanz procedure.

## 1. Exact source object

**Szondi Lipót, `Ösztön és nevelés. Kísérleti ösztönvizsgálatok ikreken`**, in: _Lélektani Tanulmányok_, vol. III, Budapest, Királyi Magyar Pázmány Péter Tudományegyetem Lélektani Intézete, 1939, pp. **79–111**.

The Hungarian Psychology History Museum gives the same record and separately lists a 1940 offprint/version:

**Szondi Lipót, `Ösztön és nevelés. Ösztöndiagnosztikai kísérletek ikreken. Közlemény a sorsanalitikus szemináriumból`, Budapest, 1940.**

An auction record for the 1940 offprint gives:

- 36 pages;
- one plate;
- printed by Mérnökök Nyomdája;
- series wording `Közlemény a Sorsanalitikus Szemináriumból`.

The relationship between the 1939 journal article and 1940 offprint is highly likely to be publication/offprint transmission of substantially the same study, but exact textual identity is **B — RECONSTRUCTED** until the objects are compared.

## 3. Why it can matter for the Inkonstanz edge case

This source predates the 1953 publication by fourteen years and is contemporary with the method-development period. It may contain:

- repeated twin test administrations;
- raw or symbolic profile series;
- early terminology for stability/change between profiles;
- worked tables or calculations;
- examples in which `±` and `0` alternate.

If the article contains a historical profile series plus any aggregate fluctuation result, it could potentially support **EUREKA-B** by reverse calculation. If it contains an early transition taxonomy or a statement attributable to the seminar methodology, it could contribute to **EUREKA-C**.

This is only an acquisition-value hypothesis. The currently exposed metadata does **not** show the classifier.

## 4. Cross-check with mature Lehrbuch

The mature _Lehrbuch der experimentellen Triebdiagnostik_ explicitly cites `Ösztön és nevelés` as the first 1939 publication of the eight-factor system. This confirms that the article is methodologically foundational, not merely pedagogical background.

This does NOT imply that Inkonstanz was already formalized there.
