# SZONDI3 — DOCTRINAL FIDELITY POLICY

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE

## Purpose

This policy governs all future extraction, modeling, review, integration and reporting involving Szondi-primary material.

The project does not modernize Szondi by subtraction. It preserves the doctrine as doctrine, including parts that are anachronistic, controversial, politically incorrect, historically dated, scientifically disputed today, or uncomfortable in contemporary clinical language.

## Core rule

For every `SZONDI_PRIMARY` passage, the project must preserve what Szondi actually claims, with its original conceptual force, conditions, distinctions and terminology.

This includes, without exception, formulations concerning:

- heredity, genes, hereditary disposition and genetic determination;
- genotropism and genotropic attraction;
- familial and ancestral transmission;
- latent/recessive familial tendencies;
- family tree, ancestry and hereditary fate;
- partner choice, friendship, profession, illness and death insofar as Szondi relates them to hereditary/familial fate;
- transgenerational patterns and intergenerational repetition in the sense used by Szondi;
- choice of spouse/partner as connected by Szondi to hereditary or familial factors;
- sexual terminology, inversion, homosexuality, bisexuality, masculinity/femininity, sadism, masochism and perversion where Szondi uses these concepts;
- criminality, psychopathy, psychosis, epilepsy, hysteria and all other diagnostic or pathodiagnostic terminology used by Szondi;
- Eros/Thanatos, Kain/Abel, Vordergänger/Hintergänger, Ich-Stufen, Triebgefahr, Schicksal and every other specifically Szondian construction.

## Non-negotiable preservation rule

No future chat, model, reviewer or implementation step may remove, soften, replace, reinterpret or silently translate away a Szondi-primary concept merely because:

- contemporary psychology uses different concepts;
- present-day genetics does not validate Szondi's genetic model;
- the term is anachronistic or politically incorrect;
- the assertion would require qualification in a modern scientific paper;
- the concept could be experienced as stigmatizing by a contemporary reader;
- another psychological school would formulate the phenomenon differently.

Such considerations belong to a separate, explicitly labeled contemporary/contextual layer. They never rewrite `SZONDI_PRIMARY`.

## Clinician-report voice — permanent non-negotiable directive

The clinician-facing Szondi report must speak **from inside Szondi's conceptual system**, not about that system in a contemporary psychological voice.

Fidelity is simultaneously:

1. **doctrinal** — preserve the concepts and relations Szondi actually states;
2. **diagnostic** — preserve concrete Szondian diagnostic/pathodiagnostic formulations when the source and executable evidence authorize them;
3. **terminological** — retain specifically Szondian vocabulary rather than replacing it with contemporary euphemisms;
4. **stylistic** — preserve, in Romanian as far as the language permits, the force, density, directness, categorical contrasts, dramatic tension and baroque clinical atmosphere characteristic of Szondi's writing.

The target is not a modernized, politically corrected, therapeutically softened or contemporary-clinical paraphrase of Szondi. The target is a faithful Szondian clinician report.

Accordingly:

- if Szondi uses a direct, harsh, pathologizing, sexual, criminological, hereditary or otherwise historically uncomfortable term and the evidence authorizes that term, the clinician report may and should use it;
- do not automatically replace `Aggressionsgefahr`, `Sadismus`, `Perversion`, `Mörder`, `Psychopathie`, hereditary/genetic formulations or comparable source language with milder contemporary descriptions merely because the original wording is uncomfortable today;
- do not insert unsolicited contemporary moral distancing, political-correctness disclaimers, therapeutic euphemisms or modern diagnostic substitutions into the Szondian report voice;
- do not make the report sound like a generic contemporary psychological report decorated with isolated German terms;
- the admitted primary corpus is both a **doctrinal authority** and the **stylistic reference corpus** for clinician-report wording;
- stylistic fidelity never licenses invented doctrine, stronger certainty, invented biography or unsupported conclusions. The source remains the ceiling.

The governing maxim is:

> **Constrain AI on doctrinal truth; do not domesticate Szondi's language.**

Or, operationally:

> **Preserve what Szondi says, preserve the diagnostic force with which he says it, and preserve how Szondi sounds when he says it — but never make Szondi say more than the source supports.**

This directive applies to future chats, prompts, synthesis contracts, report templates, AI wording layers, review criteria and release decisions. A downstream implementation that systematically sanitizes the clinician-facing Szondian voice is a fidelity defect.

## Genetics and transgenerational doctrine

The genetic, hereditary, familial and transgenerational dimension is a central part of Szondi's theoretical architecture and must not be reduced to metaphor unless Szondi himself treats it as metaphor in the cited source.

If Szondi makes a literal hereditary, genetic, familial or genotropic claim, the Primary Doctrine Registry must preserve that claim as literal Szondian doctrine. It is forbidden to silently convert it into formulations such as:

- family narrative;
- intergenerational learning;
- attachment pattern;
- family dynamics;
- symbolic inheritance;
- transmitted beliefs;
- psychological legacy;

unless the source itself authorizes that equivalence.

A contemporary layer may later evaluate the claim differently, but it must remain a separate statement with separate provenance.

## Translation rule

Romanian rendering is allowed only to make the source intelligible. It must not change doctrinal content or flatten Szondi's characteristic clinical force.

When a German term carries specifically Szondian meaning, the original German term should be retained when useful for precision or stylistic fidelity.

The Romanian formulation must preserve:

1. the object of the claim;
2. the direction of the claim;
3. the degree/force of the claim;
4. all explicit conditions and exceptions;
5. the distinction between possibility, suspicion, probability and assertion;
6. causal/genetic/hereditary language when present;
7. diagnostic and sexual terminology when present;
8. the source's characteristic directness and rhetorical force when this can be preserved without changing meaning.

## Primary Doctrine Registry rule

The Doctrine Registry records doctrine before executability.

A doctrine entry must not be weakened merely because the current engine cannot operationalize it. `NOT_EXECUTABLE_YET` is an acceptable software status; doctrinal deletion is not.

A doctrine entry may contain source contradictions, competing formulations or historically difficult content. These are data about Szondi, not errors to be silently repaired.

## Anti-inference rule

Anti-inferences belong only to the Executable Interpretation Layer and exist to prevent conclusions that Szondi does not authorize from the available evidence.

They must never be used to censor, sanitize or stylistically domesticate Szondi.

Correct: if Szondi says a configuration can indicate A only under condition B, execution may forbid concluding A when B is absent.

Incorrect: if Szondi explicitly calls a configuration `sadomasochism`, execution may not forbid the clinician report from using the term solely because it is historically sensitive.

An anti-inference must police **unsupported inference**, not contemporary discomfort. If an existing anti-inference can reasonably be read as suppressing an explicit Szondian meaning rather than preventing an unsupported extrapolation, it must be reviewed against the primary source.

## Clinician report

The clinician-facing report is the repository of full doctrinal and stylistic richness.

When supported by approved executable claims and evidence, it may and should retain Szondi's original concepts, diagnoses and formulations, including anachronistic, genetic, hereditary, sexual, pathodiagnostic, criminological and transgenerational language.

It may add provenance, configuration context, series limitations, competing Szondian interpretations, uncertainty and explicit attribution to Szondi's theoretical system when these are needed for evidential precision.

It must not replace the Szondian concept with a contemporary euphemism. It must not routinely interrupt Szondi's clinical voice with contemporary corrective commentary. Any contemporary contextualization belongs to a separate, explicitly labeled downstream layer.

AI synthesis has freedom of composition, integration and wording only inside the doctrinal/evidential envelope. It is expected to use that freedom to produce a coherent report with a recognizably Szondian conceptual and rhetorical character, not a sanitized generic report.

## Client report

The client-facing report is a separate communication layer. It may be milder, phenomenological and less technical.

This communication transformation must never alter the Doctrine Registry, executable claims or clinician-facing interpretation.

## Source conflict and modern knowledge

If Szondi-primary doctrine conflicts with contemporary evidence, the correct behavior is not to correct Szondi inside the primary doctrine entry or clinician-facing Szondian formulation.

Instead:

1. preserve the Szondi statement in `SZONDI_PRIMARY`;
2. if relevant, add a separate `CONTEMPORARY_CONTEXT` statement;
3. preserve both provenances;
4. do not let the contemporary layer silently overwrite the primary layer.

## Audit failure criteria

A Szondi-primary representation or clinician-report implementation fails fidelity if it:

- omits a substantive element because it is historically uncomfortable;
- replaces a Szondian term with a modern psychological term without source support;
- converts a literal hereditary/genetic/genotropic statement into metaphor;
- suppresses sexual/pathological/criminological terminology present in the source;
- changes the strength of the source assertion;
- adds a contemporary correction inside the primary doctrinal content;
- uses an anti-inference to negate or sanitize an explicit source meaning;
- merges doctrine with executable conditions in a way that makes the source statement disappear when execution is blocked;
- systematically rewrites the clinician report into politically corrected, euphemistic, generic contemporary clinical prose;
- preserves concepts while discarding the characteristic force and style of Szondi when faithful Romanian wording can preserve them;
- imitates a dramatic Szondian style by inventing claims, diagnoses or certainty not supported by the source.

## Final rule for future chats

> **When working from Szondi-primary sources, fidelity takes precedence over modernization. Preserve the doctrine, concrete diagnostic language, terminology and characteristic Szondian clinical voice first. The clinician report must speak from inside Szondi's system and must not be sanitized for contemporary taste or political correctness. Any contemporary critique, translation, contextualization or softening must be separate, labeled and downstream; it may never rewrite the primary doctrine or clinician-facing Szondian interpretation. Constrain AI on doctrinal truth; do not domesticate Szondi's language.**
