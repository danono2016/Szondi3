## Core doctrinal objects

### Three research domains of the unconscious

U000497-U000500 place Szondi's Schicksalsanalyse beside the personally repressed unconscious of Freud and the collective unconscious of Jung. Szondi calls Schicksalsanalyse the third research direction and identifies its specific object as `familiäres Unbewußtes`.

At U000499 he states that suppressed `Ahnenansprüche` determine the individual's `Schicksal` through unconsciously guided `Wahlhandlungen` in love, friendship, profession, illness and death. The same unit defines `Schicksal` as an integration of `Leib und Seele`, `Erbe und Trieb`, `Ich und Geist`, and `Diesseitigkeit und Jenseitigkeit`, and names the resulting fate science `dialektische Anankologie`.

U000500 further states that the familial unconscious conditions `Wahlschicksal`, interpersonal relations and thereby the fate of the community.

### Schicksalsanalyse as dualistic humane genetics

U000510 gives the compact definition: Schicksalsanalyse is the dualistically constructed `humane Genetik` of the unconscious.

U000511 then develops that genetics at full historical source strength. Szondi says Schicksalsanalyse **attempted to prove** (`versuchte ... zu beweisen`) that hereditary dispositions conditioning mental illness and other psychological disturbances are `doppelt angelegt`: an illness disposition is paired with a corresponding disposition to `höhere Geistesfähigkeiten`. The registry does not upgrade this attempted-proof wording into a claim of completed proof.

The same unit describes human psychological-spiritual hereditary dispositions as having a `Janusgesicht` and explicitly pairs:
- `Epilepsie ↔ Religion-Theologie`;
- `Schizophrenie ↔ Psychiatrie, Psychopathologie`;
- `manisch-depressives Irresein ↔ Kunst (Malerei)`.

These pairings are preserved without modernization or sanitization.

### Ich as chooser among dualistically directed dispositions

Still at U000511, Szondi says `u. E.` that the `Ich` is the instance choosing between the dualistically directed dispositions, alongside environmental and aptitude factors. The explicit `u. E.` qualifier is preserved as `ASSUMPTION`.

The canonical OCR renders the final `Ich` as `Zch`; the admitted PDF, printed p. 22, confirms `Ich`.

### Unconscious choice and Schicksalskrankheiten

U000511 characterizes Schicksalsanalyse as a `Psychologie unbewußter Wahlhandlungen` and locates its particular field in `familiäres Unbewußtes`, whose principal function Szondi identifies in choice acts.

The same passage gives the principal application domain as `Schicksalskrankheiten` / `Wahlkrankheiten`, explicitly including disturbances of unconscious choice in love, friendship and profession, `Wahl der Kriminalitätsformen`, `Psychosen-`, `Neurosenwahl`, psychosomatic illness choice and choice of particular forms of death (`Unfall, Mord, Selbstmord`). These pathodiagnostic and criminological formulations are retained literally in doctrinal force.

### The three languages of the unconscious

U000514-U000517 state that the unconscious speaks three languages:
1. `Sprache der Symptome` → conflicts of the personally repressed unconscious;
2. `Sprache der Symbole` → archetypal processes of the collective unconscious;
3. `Sprache der Wahl` → `Ahnenansprüche` and `Ahnenfiguren` of the familial unconscious.

U000518 adds the programmatic rule that a depth psychology of the whole unconscious must learn all three languages.
