## Scope

This batch covers Szondi's explicit application of quantitative gene theory and polyallelie to Schicksalsanalyse. It preserves the theory of `Triebgene`, their factor-specific Triebanlagen, their polyallelic variation, and the asserted quantitative continuity between normal, neurotic/psychopathic/characterological and extreme psychiatric manifestations.

## Fundamental quantitative thesis

U000468 states as a `grundsätzliche Annahme der Schicksalsanalyse` that the difference between `Geistes-Kranken` and `Geistes-Gesunden` is quantitative rather than qualitative. U000469 then states that the genes determining genuine `idiopathische Geisteskrankheit` are primarily specific `Triebgene`, present in every human being without exception, and that their `Quantität` / `Dosierung` decides whether the person becomes `geistesgesund` or `geisteskrank`.

These statements are preserved at their full source strength as Szondi's assumptions. They are not modernized, softened, or converted into contemporary genetic claims.

## Triebgene and the eight factors

U000470 defines `Triebgene` as specific hereditary factors determining `Triebhandlungen` and the collective recurrent forms of instinctual reaction. Each Triebgen / Trieballel is treated as the seat of a specific `Triebanlage`.

The factor associations are retained without euphemistic replacement:

- `h` — Hermaphroditismus; Triebanlage zur Bisexualität;
- `s` — Sadismus;
- `e` — Epilepsie; maximal accumulation of Wut, Haß, Zorn and Rache followed by sudden explosive discharge;
- `hy` — Hysterie; `sich zur Schau zu tragen`;
- `p` — Paranoia and the expansive tendency of the Ich;
- `k` — Katatonie and the restrictive tendency of the Ich;
- `d` — Depression and seeking a new object after loss;
- `m` — Manie and clinging to the old object.

The hereditary, sexual and pathodiagnostic content is retained as doctrine-source content. No P2B inference rule is created from it.

## Polyallelic Triebgene

U000471-U000475 state that Triebbedürfnisse are hereditarily determined by theoretically assumed Triebgene; each Triebgen has multiple allele variations at the same chromosomal locus and therefore a `multiple Allel-Reihe / Polyallelie`. Different allelic variants of the same Triebgen produce different manifestations within the same `Triebkreis`.

Szondi explicitly adopts Goldschmidt's quantitative theory and assumes a quantitatively graded allele series for each Triebgen. A quantitative difference of the same gene can reach a point where it appears phenotypically as another property.

The exact symbolic p-allele dominance/valence formula in U000475-U000477 remains OCR-sensitive. The doctrinal proposition — a p-Alleireihe with `quantitativ abgestufter Valenz` — is preserved, but the precise formula typography is not invented. This is the only unresolved symbol-level ambiguity in the batch.

## p and k: normal-to-pathological series

For `p`, Szondi states that paranoid schizophrenia / Bleuler's `Paranoid` is one manifestation of the same Triebgen whose quantitatively normal dose determines the physiological tendency of every person to expand the Person / Ich.

For `k`, Katatonie is assigned to variants of the same Triebgen whose normal quantity conditions the physiological tendency to restrict the Ich against expansion tendencies.

U000479-U000480 place between the normal and `extrem-krankhaft` p/k manifestations an entire series of `paranoide bzw. schizoide Neurosen, Psychopathien, Charaktervariationen`, all conceived as determined by special allele variations of the same p or k Triebgen.

U000481 then derives the concluding assertion: because members of a polyallelic Triebgen series differ quantitatively, Schicksalsanalyse maintains that the difference between `Geisteskranken` and `Geistesgesunden` is quantitative, not qualitative.
