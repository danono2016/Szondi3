## Core doctrinal objects

### Glaubensfunktion as an integrative addition

U000576 says that Schicksalsanalyse approaches the relation between depth psychology and religion not by `Umwertung`, but by `Ergänzung` and therefore `Integration`. Szondi says it supplements the Freudian Ich-Analyse with the `Glaubensfunktion des Ichs`.

The same unit defines faith as a special mode of mystical participation and, in the printed text, as `ein Schicksal des ichhaften Partizipationsstrebens`: the believer is driven by the primordial participation striving to be one, equal, related and united with a supra-personal and supernatural spiritual instance. Canonical OCR damages this phrase, so the admitted PDF was used only to arbitrate the printed wording.

### Positive mystische participatio perennis

U000577 gives Szondi's explicit compact definition: `Glaube` is a positive, mystical `participatio perennis` in which the Ego unites with a spiritual instance and transfers its own omnipotence (`Allmacht`) to it.

U000578 then states that faith is an `Ich-Funktion`, that the birth of religion is conditioned by the existence of the Ego, and that without the Ego there is no faith. The transfer of omnipotence to `Geist` is attributed to the Ego: in faith the Ego transfers its own `Macht zum Sein` to the highest spiritual instance.

The same paragraph explicitly limits the psychological claim. Szondi says this transfer theory does not mean that the highest spiritual instance is not omnipotent in itself without the transfer; he assigns that question to theology. This boundary is preserved without converting the passage into a theological assertion.

### Five conditions of geistige Glaubensfunktion

U000579-U000580 set out five conditions:

1. existence of the highest spiritual instance;
2. existence of the Ego;
3. opening of the Ego upward to Spirit — `apertura ad coelum`;
4. `Allmachtübertragung` of the Ego to Spirit;
5. realization of positive participation with Spirit in life and behavior.

Szondi then states that if one of these conditions is absent, or if one of the conditioning existences is doubted by reason, the Glaubensfunktion is disturbed. The following stronger identification is explicitly qualified `u. E.`: disturbances of Glaubensfunktion are, in Szondi's view, disturbances of Ich-Funktion. The registry therefore records this latter claim as `ASSUMPTION`, not as an unqualified assertion.

### Programmatic integration stance

U000600 explains that the currents discussed were selected because they supply `brauchbare Bausteine` for integration. U000601 adds Szondi's explicit programmatic position that Freudian psychoanalysis `ist und bleibt auch in der Zukunft der Hauptstrom in der Tiefenpsychologie`. The latter phrase is OCR-corrupted in canonical and was visually confirmed on printed p. 32.
