# Inkonstanzmethode — Russian / post-Soviet Szondi tradition sweep, 2026-09-05

**Status:** RESEARCH / EXTERNAL SECONDARY EVIDENCE ONLY  
**Purpose:** mine Russian-language and post-Soviet Szondi teaching materials, forums, cached lecture notes and manuals for fragments that could clarify the unresolved Böszörményi transition `± <-> 0` and the historical classifier.

## 1. Why the Russian/post-Soviet tradition matters

The Russian-language Szondi tradition is not negligible. Public sources show:

- multiple Russian editions/translations of Szondi;
- practitioner teaching lineages in Moscow and Ekaterinburg;
- references to training by Szondi Institute figures Friedjung Jüttner and Heidi Achtnich;
- contemporary fate-analysis groups and lecture archives;
- Russian-language manuals that preserve older technical distinctions not always visible in current Western summaries.

This makes Russian caches/manuals a legitimate high-yield search layer for derivative quotations and period terminology, while remaining secondary evidence.

## 2. Russian teaching material recovered

### A. A. V. Smirnov, Ekaterinburg lectures

A large public slide transcription, `Екатеринбургские лекции по экспериментальной диагностике побуждений Леопольда Зонди`, survives online. Its historical notes say Russian specialists had direct training contact with Szondi Institute teachers and preserve a substantial technical teaching corpus.

No explicit `Inkonstanzmethode` / `qu-t-c` formula has yet surfaced in the indexed text.

### B. L. N. Sobchik / Russian portrait-choice teaching

Russian derivative teaching texts based on Sobchik preserve a strong distinction between ambivalent and null reactions:

- stable `±` = a tension that is recognized but not yet realized;
- `0` = external realization/discharge;
- the text explicitly calls a zero reaction arising after ambivalence **post-ambivalent** (`постамбивалентная`);
- it says opposite tendencies can paralyze one another and thereby yield a null reaction.

This material does **not** recover Böszörményi's multiplier classes and is partly Sobchik's own interpretive tradition. It therefore cannot be used as direct evidence for `t`.

However, it is relevant to the unresolved edge because it preserves the same dynamic intuition seen in Deri/Mélon: `± -> 0` may represent a qualitative transition from tension/ambivalence to discharge rather than a merely quantitative within-family shift.

Thus Russian secondary tradition adds indirect conceptual support to the **t-hypothesis**, or at minimum weakens any argument that `±` and `0` must be treated as dynamically near-identical merely because they share a formal family.

## 3. Important Russian formal table clue

A Russian derivative source reproduces a mature reaction table in which mixed distributions such as `4/2`, `3/2`, `2/3`, `2/4` are described as ambivalent, with only equal mixed distributions called `амбиэквальные` (ambiequal).

This is useful mainly as a warning:

- Russian later teaching generally follows mature Szondi reaction classification;
- therefore printed `±` in Russian derivative material cannot be assumed to follow Böszörményi's stricter historical equality convention;
- any Russian source useful for the Inkonstanz classifier must explicitly discuss Böszörményi or an older historical rule.

Do **not** use the mature Russian table to back-fill Böszörményi.

## 4. Naming/transliteration problem

Web search is hampered by unstable Cyrillic transliteration of Böszörményi. Searches were run across forms including:

- Бёсёрмени
- Бёсёрменьи
- Бесермени
- Босормени / Босорменьи
- Böszörményi
- Boszormenyi

No Russian page has yet surfaced that explicitly names the Inkonstanzmethode or reproduces its `i/qu/t/c` table.

This means future Russian mining should rely less on the author's surname and more on translated conceptual phrases:

- `факторные колебания`
- `изменчивость факторных реакций`
- `непостоянство факторных реакций`
- `изменение направления реакции`

## 5. Russian institutional / archive leads

### A. International Fate-Analytical Community / Russian fate-analysis institute

The site `maltsev-worldwide.com` hosts an active Russian-language fate-analysis corpus and free translations of some Szondi works. Initial search has not exposed Inkonstanz arithmetic, but it is a high-value archive target because it republishes old lecture/research material and may contain untranslated technical vocabulary.

### B. Contemporary Russian Szondi lecture archives

A current Telegram/public lecture community (`Глубины групповой психологии`) explicitly says it records and archives courses on Szondi and runs fate-analytically oriented supervision/lecture groups. This is not historical evidence itself, but it establishes a living Russian practitioner network where older training notes may survive.

### C. Russian publication line

Public catalogues confirm several Russian editions of Szondi, including `Учебник экспериментальной диагностики влечений` and `Экспериментальная диагностика побуждений`. Some are translations from earlier English/German editions. Because the Inkonstanz section enters later editions, edition lineage must be checked before treating a Russian book as evidence.

## 6. Current evidential effect on `± <-> 0`

Russian/post-Soviet material does **not** close the edge case.

What it adds is a meaningful conceptual asymmetry:

- `±` is repeatedly taught as retained tension/ambivalence;
- `0` is repeatedly taught as discharge/realization/opening;
- some Russian texts explicitly describe zero as post-ambivalent.

This aligns with Deri and later Mélon in treating `± <-> 0` as potentially a qualitative change of state.

Therefore the Russian tradition currently gives **indirect support to `t`**, not enough to outweigh Janssen's literal wording but enough to make `qu` unsafe as a default reconstruction.

Best current state remains:

`± <-> 0 = UNRESOLVED`.

## Verdict

The Russian tradition is worth systematic mining and should remain an active search front. It has already contributed one important conceptual clue — explicit post-ambivalent zero / tension-to-discharge language — but no source has yet recovered the missing Böszörményi transition class. Treat all current Russian clues as **secondary conceptual evidence**, not implementation authority.
