## Scope

This source-order batch covers the subsection *Die Wirkung der latenten, rezessiven Gene* and stops immediately before §6 *Die Heterosis*. It preserves the historical genetic scaffold separately from Szondi's own Schicksalsanalyse-specific use of `Latenz`.

Szondi gives three reasons/senses for retaining `latent`: (1) a recessive hereditary disposition can be invisibly present in the person's `Erbschatz`; (2) `genotypische Unsichtbarkeit` does not exclude another manifestation, explicitly including `genotropistisch`; and (3) psychologically, the `Triebansprüche` belonging to latent recessive genes are `unbewußt`, hence latent relative to consciousness. The registry preserves these separately rather than collapsing them into one modernized definition.

The batch then preserves Szondi's argument about biological activity in heterozygosity. The historical `scheintot` position is recorded because it is part of the source argument, but it is not mislabeled as Szondi's accepted proposition: U000319 explicitly says that this long-standing assumption was later regarded as false. The competing account retained by Szondi is that latent genes preserve a specific trait-forming effect with attenuated manifestation valence. Subsequent historical animal and human examples are preserved as the evidence Szondi invokes for that account.
