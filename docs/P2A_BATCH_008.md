## Quantitative gene theory

U000438-U000453 develop the historical genetic framework used by Szondi. The registry keeps attribution explicit where the source is reporting Goldschmidt or the genetics of the period.

The main propositions preserved are:

- manifestation of an `Erbanlage` depends not only on qualitative factor properties and environment but also on the quantitative relation to the allele partner and the wider `Genmilieu`;
- Heterosis is presented as resting on the assumption that `Krankheit/Tod` and `Hypervitalität` can depend on different quantities of the same hereditary factor;
- in Goldschmidt's sex-differentiation formulation, both F and M factors are present and actual manifestation depends on their quantitative relation;
- hereditary factors involved in sex differentiation differ in `Quantität` / `Valenz`;
- the same gene may have a quantitatively graded series of variations producing graded phenotypic expressions;
- `multiple Allele` are defined as a series of allelic genes of the same hereditary factor at the same chromosomal locus;
- Goldschmidt's multiple allelomorphs are presented as different quantitative states of one factor pair;
- Dominanz and Rezessivität are treated as relative relations of `Gen-Valenz` / `Gen-Quantität`, not fixed absolute properties;
- Szondi summarizes the development by saying the question of dominance and recessivity became a question of gene quantity.

Exact formula typography in the illustrative allele series is not silently reconstructed where the canonical OCR is symbolically unstable. The conceptual propositions remain fully intelligible and are registered without inventing the missing typography.

## Disease, death, health and allele variation

U000453 records an `Annahme` of special importance for Szondi's later use: hereditary factors leading to disease and death are conceived as mutations of the same gene that determines healthy manifestation, with a series of intermediate variations between extreme alleles.

The Hammerschlag hearing example then gives a concrete hereditary model. As presented by Szondi, an `O-Gen` determining hearing has a polyallelic series whose different mutations correspond to forms of `Heredopathia acustica`; different allelic combinations are said to produce different phenotypic forms.

U000462 is Szondi's own further `Annahme`: `Musikalität`, described as `extrem-positive Gehör`, may be an extreme allele mutation of the same O-gene whose polar-opposite mutation determines hereditary inner-ear disease. The full hereditary and pathodiagnostic force of this claim is retained.

## Günther Just and the research horizon

U000464 quotes Günther Just describing quantitative gene action and multiple allelomorphs in humans as nearly a `terra incognita`, while also identifying them as a promising future research field. The quotation explicitly seeks a physiological understanding of the processes behind Dominanz and Rezessivität of a `krankhafte Anlage`, beyond purely statistical-Mendelian analysis.

U000465 then gives Szondi's explicit bridge: `Das ist der Punkt, wo die Theorie der Schicksalsanalyse mit der Genquantitätstheorie in Berührung kommt.`
