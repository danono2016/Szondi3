## 1. New operational result: Szondi's 1939 laboratory report is available in full reprint

A full reprint of Szondi Lipót's short contemporary report

**"Beszámoló a M. kir. Állami Gyógypedagógiai Tanárképző Főiskola Kórtani és Gyógytani Laboratóriumának 1938. évi munkásságáról"**

(originally _Jövő Útjain_, 1939, 1–2, pp. 3–5)

is reproduced in _Gyógypedagógiai Szemle_ 21/3 (1993), pp. 201–204.

This matters because Karl Bürgi-Meyer explicitly says that information he obtained from **Lőrincz Ákosné Sófalvy Rózsa 1939** is in many respects identical with Szondi's 1939 report. The inaccessible Lőrincz article therefore has a partially recoverable contemporary parallel/control text.

## 2. What the 1939 Szondi report actually says

The report enumerates the laboratory's active program for 1938:

1. family registry / heredity research;
2. drive-theoretical and drive-pathological research for education;
3. experimental drive diagnostics (`ösztön-próba`, `genoteszt`);
4. experimental matching of educators and children by drive group;
5. vocational drive diagnostics.

Crucially, Szondi says that the genotest is administered in **"többször megismételt kísérletekben"** (repeated experiments) to obtain a drive profile. Thus repeated administration is already explicitly part of the laboratory's procedure at this early stage.

However, the reprinted report contains **no explicit discussion of factorial fluctuation scoring**, no `Inkonstanz`, no transition taxonomy, no `qu/t/c`, and no named Böszörményi contribution.

Targeted text checks in the reprint found no occurrence of:

- `Böszörményi`;
- `ingado...`;
- `változ...` in the relevant reprinted report;
- any table/classification of changes between repeated profiles.

### Epistemic effect

**A — SOURCE-VERIFIED:** repeated Szondi testing was explicitly part of the Budapest laboratory procedure by the 1938/1939 report stage.

**A — SOURCE-VERIFIED negative content for this specific short report:** the reprinted text itself does not present the later Böszörményi classifier.

This does **not** prove that Lőrincz 1939 lacks additional details, because Bürgi-Meyer says the two accounts are similar "in many respects", not identical word-for-word.

Effect on `± <-> 0`: **neutral**.
