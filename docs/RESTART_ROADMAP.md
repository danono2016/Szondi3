# SZONDI3 — CLEAN RESTART ROADMAP

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE DEVELOPMENT ORDER

## Principle

Szondi3 is a clean restart built from admitted evidence and Szondi3 specifications.

`Sources -> Canonical Access -> Deterministic Test Engine -> Primary Doctrine -> Executable Interpretation -> Clinical Evidence -> Integration -> Reports -> Validation`

A later phase may not justify assumptions in an earlier phase. Legacy projects are outside the development and validation path: they are not source truth, design authority, implementation templates or gates.

## P0 — Constitution + Sources

Establish the immutable documentary basis.

Required:
- constitutional/source-layer rules fixed;
- admitted DOCX/PDF and 48 stimulus binaries identity-verified;
- canonical access independently implemented and reproducible;
- visual/source limitations recorded explicitly;
- stimulus series/position/factor mapping established from primary Szondi evidence;
- photographed-person historical metadata excluded from runtime.

Gate: `P0_SOURCES_PASS`.

PASS means the corpus and stimulus assets are present, identity-verifiable, reproducibly accessible, separated by doctrinal layer, and sufficient to begin deterministic administration/scoring work.

## P1 — Deterministic Test Engine

Reconstruct source-authorized formal procedures in this order:
1. stimulus identity and series presentation;
2. administration protocol;
3. VGP selection/recording;
4. EKP/background procedure;
5. factor counts and reactions;
6. vectors/profiles and quantum intensities;
7. complements and repeated series;
8. formal indices/classifications;
9. remaining source-authorized deterministic procedures.

Rules: specify from admitted sources before implementation; turn source examples/invariants into tests; preserve ambiguity explicitly; no clinical interpretation and no photographed-person metadata.

Gate: `P1_DETERMINISTIC_ENGINE_PASS`.

## P2A — Primary Doctrine Registry

Preserve what Szondi says before deciding what software can infer. Keep exact provenance, original terminology, assertion strength, conditions/exceptions, genetic/hereditary/genotropic content, sexual/pathodiagnostic terminology, and contradictions. Szondi-primary and post-Szondian material remain separate.

Gate: `P2A_PRIMARY_DOCTRINE_PASS`.

## P2B — Executable Interpretation

Derive activatable claims from doctrine without rewriting it. Triggers and context must be explicit; uncertainty cannot be inflated; every executable claim links back to doctrine/source.

Gate: `P2B_EXECUTABLE_INTERPRETATION_PASS`.

## P3 — Clinical Evidence

Represent protocol facts, activated interpretations, contradictions and provenance in one traceable structure.

Gate: `P3_CLINICAL_GRAPH_PASS`.

## P4 — Integration

Combine evidence without inventing reconciliation or increasing certainty.

Gate: `P4_INTEGRATION_PASS`.

## P5 — Dual Reports

Clinician report preserves full authorized Szondian richness. Client report is a separate downstream communication transformation and may be milder without rewriting doctrine.

Gate: `P5_REPORT_PASS`.

## P6 — Validation + Release

Validate end-to-end administration, scoring, doctrine fidelity, executable interpretation, provenance, ambiguity preservation, integration, reporting, privacy and regression protection.

Gate: `P6_RELEASE_PASS`.

## Repair rule

Errors are repaired in the layer where they occur.

> **Never repair executable logic by rewriting the source doctrine it was supposed to represent.**
