## Scope

This batch begins `ERSTER TEIL — TRIEBLEHRE UND METHODIK`, `Kapitel I — Das menschliche Triebsystem`, and preserves Szondi's foundational distinctions among Instinkthandlung, Triebhandlung, Triebtendenz/Triebstrebung, Triebbedürfnis/Triebfaktor and Trieb/Triebvektor.

The batch is especially important for later cross-source integration with `Ich-Analyse`, because Szondi explicitly says at U000626 that this practical Lehrbuch is theoretically supplemented by `Triebanalyse` and `Ich-Analyse`. The present entries therefore preserve Lehrbuch's own formulations without importing later or more elaborate Ich-Analyse doctrine into them.

## Core doctrinal objects

### Triebe as Radikale

U000617-U000624 present drives as `Radikale` / roots of human action and existence. Szondi treats human `Triebfaktoren` as unhistorical, hereditary and permanently active roots whose manifestations vary while the root remains the same. The hereditary language is retained source-near rather than replaced by modern family-pattern language.

### Instinkthandlung versus Triebhandlung

U000628-U000630 distinguish strict hereditary instinctual sequences from the more plastic `Triebhandlungen`. Instinctive action is presented as governed by an inherited fixed route, whereas drive action is only partly inherited and can be modified by individual learning, exercise and adaptation.

### Human drive life requires Ego drives

U000632 gives a general thesis of the Lehrbuch: human `Triebleben` is always a product of the interaction of animal `Triebbedürfnisse` and human `Ich-Triebe`. Szondi explicitly warns that ignoring this thesis in experimental drive diagnosis causes misinterpretation.

U000633 introduces `Rand-Mitte-Methode` accordingly: `Rand` = animal drive needs; `Mitte` = human Ego/Superego functions.

### Genetic definition of Triebtendenz

U000635-U000637 define `Triebtendenz` / `Triebstrebung` as the genetically smallest drive unit, determined by one gene / hereditary disposition, paternal or maternal. Szondi says tendencies are inherited as paired allelic parental tendencies and together form a `Triebbedürfnis`.

### Triebbedürfnis / Triebfaktor

U000638-U000639 define a drive need/factor as the product of two related allelic tendencies, often polar opposites. Their inherited quantitative and qualitative differences are presented as producing the variety of the manifest need.

The Männlichkeit example is preserved at full source strength: Szondi couples activity/aggression/sadism with passivity/devotion/masochism and presents the two poles as hereditary parental tendencies. No modernization or sanitization has been applied.

### Trieb / Triebvektor

U000640 defines a drive/vector as the interweaving of two cooperative drive factors following one unified biopsychological goal. U000641-U000644 then give source-level definitions of Sexualtrieb, Kontakttrieb, Ich-Trieb and Paroxysmaltrieb.

For `Ich-Trieb`, U000643 explicitly links `Egodiastole / Ich-Erweiterung` and `Egosystole / Ich-Einengung`, aimed at securing the unity and health of unconscious Ego life. This entry is deliberately kept as Lehrbuch doctrine; the independent Ich-Analyse corpus can later qualify or extend it through cross-source relations.

### General traits of drives

U000645 assigns two general traits to all drives: they condition actions and behaviors rather than affects or intellectual activities, and their single goal is satisfaction through discharge of their dynamics whether they remain unconscious or become conscious.
