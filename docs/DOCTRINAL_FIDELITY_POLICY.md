# SZONDI3 — DOCTRINAL FIDELITY POLICY

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE

## Purpose

This policy governs all future extraction, modeling, review, integration and reporting involving Szondi-primary material.

The project does not modernize Szondi by subtraction. It preserves the doctrine as doctrine, including parts that are anachronistic, controversial, politically incorrect, historically dated, scientifically disputed today, or uncomfortable in contemporary clinical language.

## Core rule

For every `SZONDI_PRIMARY` passage, the project must preserve what Szondi actually claims, with its original conceptual force, conditions, distinctions and terminology.

This includes, without exception, formulations concerning:

- heredity, genes, hereditary disposition and genetic determination;
- genotropism and genotropic attraction;
- familial and ancestral transmission;
- latent/recessive familial tendencies;
- family tree, ancestry and hereditary fate;
- partner choice, friendship, profession, illness and death insofar as Szondi relates them to hereditary/familial fate;
- transgenerational patterns and intergenerational repetition in the sense used by Szondi;
- choice of spouse/partner as connected by Szondi to hereditary or familial factors;
- sexual terminology, inversion, homosexuality, bisexuality, masculinity/femininity, sadism, masochism and perversion where Szondi uses these concepts;
- criminality, psychopathy, psychosis, epilepsy, hysteria and all other diagnostic or pathodiagnostic terminology used by Szondi;
- Eros/Thanatos, Kain/Abel, Vordergänger/Hintergänger, Ich-Stufen, Triebgefahr, Schicksal and every other specifically Szondian construction.

## Non-negotiable preservation rule

No future chat, model, reviewer or implementation step may remove, soften, replace, reinterpret or silently translate away a Szondi-primary concept merely because:

- contemporary psychology uses different concepts;
- present-day genetics does not validate Szondi's genetic model;
- the term is anachronistic or politically incorrect;
- the assertion would require qualification in a modern scientific paper;
- the concept could be experienced as stigmatizing by a contemporary reader;
- another psychological school would formulate the phenomenon differently.

Such considerations belong to a separate, explicitly labeled contemporary/contextual layer. They never rewrite `SZONDI_PRIMARY`.

## Genetics and transgenerational doctrine

The genetic, hereditary, familial and transgenerational dimension is a central part of Szondi's theoretical architecture and must not be reduced to metaphor unless Szondi himself treats it as metaphor in the cited source.

If Szondi makes a literal hereditary, genetic, familial or genotropic claim, the Primary Doctrine Registry must preserve that claim as literal Szondian doctrine. It is forbidden to silently convert it into formulations such as:

- family narrative;
- intergenerational learning;
- attachment pattern;
- family dynamics;
- symbolic inheritance;
- transmitted beliefs;
- psychological legacy;

unless the source itself authorizes that equivalence.

A contemporary layer may later evaluate the claim differently, but it must remain a separate statement with separate provenance.

## Translation rule

Romanian rendering is allowed only to make the source intelligible. It must not change doctrinal content.

When a German term carries specifically Szondian meaning, the original German term should be retained when useful for precision.

The Romanian formulation must preserve:

1. the object of the claim;
2. the direction of the claim;
3. the degree/force of the claim;
4. all explicit conditions and exceptions;
5. the distinction between possibility, suspicion, probability and assertion;
6. causal/genetic/hereditary language when present;
7. diagnostic and sexual terminology when present.

## Primary Doctrine Registry rule

The Doctrine Registry records doctrine before executability.

A doctrine entry must not be weakened merely because the current engine cannot operationalize it. `NOT_EXECUTABLE_YET` is an acceptable software status; doctrinal deletion is not.

A doctrine entry may contain source contradictions, competing formulations or historically difficult content. These are data about Szondi, not errors to be silently repaired.

## Anti-inference rule

Anti-inferences belong only to the Executable Interpretation Layer and exist to prevent conclusions that Szondi does not authorize from the available evidence.

They must never be used to censor Szondi.

Correct: if Szondi says a configuration can indicate A only under condition B, execution may forbid concluding A when B is absent.

Incorrect: if Szondi explicitly calls a configuration `sadomasochism`, execution may not forbid the clinician report from using the term solely because it is historically sensitive.

## Clinician report

The clinician-facing report is the repository of full doctrinal richness.

When supported by approved executable claims and evidence, it may and should retain Szondi's original concepts, including anachronistic, genetic, hereditary, sexual, pathodiagnostic and transgenerational formulations.

It may add provenance, configuration context, series limitations, competing Szondian interpretations, uncertainty and explicit attribution to Szondi's theoretical system.

It must not replace the Szondian concept with a contemporary euphemism.

## Client report

The client-facing report is a separate communication layer. It may be milder, phenomenological and less technical.

This communication transformation must never alter the Doctrine Registry, executable claims or clinician-facing interpretation.

## Source conflict and modern knowledge

If Szondi-primary doctrine conflicts with contemporary evidence, the correct behavior is not to correct Szondi inside the primary doctrine entry.

Instead:

1. preserve the Szondi statement in `SZONDI_PRIMARY`;
2. if relevant, add a separate `CONTEMPORARY_CONTEXT` statement;
3. preserve both provenances;
4. do not let the contemporary layer silently overwrite the primary layer.

## Audit failure criteria

A Szondi-primary representation fails doctrinal fidelity if it:

- omits a substantive element because it is historically uncomfortable;
- replaces a Szondian term with a modern psychological term without source support;
- converts a literal hereditary/genetic/genotropic statement into metaphor;
- suppresses sexual/pathological terminology present in the source;
- changes the strength of the source assertion;
- adds a contemporary correction inside the primary doctrinal content;
- uses an anti-inference to negate an explicit source meaning;
- merges doctrine with executable conditions in a way that makes the source statement disappear when execution is blocked.

## Final rule for future chats

> **When working from Szondi-primary sources, fidelity takes precedence over modernization. Preserve the doctrine first, including genetics, heredity, genotropism, family fate and transgenerational claims exactly in their Szondian sense. Any contemporary critique, translation or softening must be separate, labeled and downstream; it may never rewrite the primary doctrine.**
