# SZONDI3 — CLEAN RESTART ROADMAP

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE DEVELOPMENT ORDER

## Governing principle

Szondi3 is a total software restart. Development proceeds from admitted evidence and source-derived specifications, not from predecessor implementation.

Canonical direction:

`Sources -> Canonical Access -> Deterministic Test Engine -> Primary Doctrine Registry -> Executable Interpretation -> Clinical Evidence/Graph -> Integration -> Reports -> Validation`

A later phase may not be used to justify assumptions in an earlier phase.

---

## P0 — CONSTITUTION + SOURCES

### Purpose
Establish the epistemic constitution and immutable documentary basis before executable software exists.

### Required work
- constitutional policies authoritative and mutually consistent;
- authorized source set inventoried;
- original DOCX/PDF binaries admitted and identity-verified;
- 48 stimulus binaries admitted and identity-verified;
- photograph metadata boundary fixed;
- new canonical extraction/access pipeline designed and implemented from zero;
- deterministic canonical regeneration verified;
- comparison against predecessor canonical hashes performed only after independent generation;
- source limitations recorded, including missing visual PDF arbitration where applicable.

### Gate: `P0_SOURCES_PASS`
PASS only when the documentary corpus and stimulus assets are present in Szondi3, identity-verifiable, reproducibly accessible and unambiguously separated by doctrinal layer.

No clinical interpretation code is permitted before this gate.

---

## P1 — DETERMINISTIC TEST ENGINE

### Purpose
Reconstruct from primary sources all formal administration, scoring and series procedures that are algorithmically authorized.

### Working order
1. stimulus identity and series presentation;
2. basic administration protocol;
3. VGP selection/recording;
4. EKP/background procedure;
5. factor counts;
6. primary reaction table and quantum intensities;
7. vectors and profiles;
8. complements;
9. repeated series;
10. formal indices and classifications;
11. all remaining source-authorized deterministic procedures.

### Rules
- every rule is specified from source before implementation;
- source examples and invariants become tests before or together with code;
- Szondi2 may be compared only after independent implementation and is `ORACLE_ONLY`;
- disagreement with predecessor behavior triggers source review;
- no clinical interpretation belongs in P1;
- no historical photograph metadata belongs in P1.

### Gate: `P1_DETERMINISTIC_ENGINE_PASS`
PASS requires deterministic reproducibility, source provenance for every implemented procedure, explicit handling of source ambiguity/unfinished procedures and an inventory proving that major formal procedures were not silently omitted.

---

## P2A — PRIMARY DOCTRINE REGISTRY

### Purpose
Preserve what Szondi says before asking what software can infer.

### Unit of work
A doctrine entry is source-near and does not require an executable trigger.

It must preserve, as applicable:
- exact source provenance;
- original terminology;
- Romanian rendering without doctrinal modernization;
- object and direction of assertion;
- assertion strength;
- conditions/exceptions;
- genetic/hereditary/genotropic/etiological content;
- sexual/pathodiagnostic terminology;
- configuration/series context stated by source;
- related/competing/contradictory source passages.

### Rules
- doctrine may be non-executable;
- doctrine may contain ambiguity or internal tension;
- source-era terminology is data, not an error;
- no anti-inference mechanism exists in this layer;
- Szondi-primary and post-Szondian doctrine live in distinct namespaces/layers.

### Gate: `P2A_PRIMARY_DOCTRINE_PASS`
PASS requires sufficient primary-source coverage for the intended complete interpretation and clinician review that representation has not sanitized, modernized or structurally distorted Szondi.

---

## P2B — EXECUTABLE INTERPRETATION LAYER

### Purpose
Derive formally activatable interpretations from Doctrine Registry entries without rewriting them.

### Rules
- every executable claim references one or more doctrine entries;
- trigger/context/series/intensity conditions are explicit;
- ambiguity remains unresolved where source discrimination is unavailable;
- anti-inferences constrain only unsupported application, never source doctrine;
- no executable claim can increase source assertion strength;
- claims remain atomic relative to their trigger;
- post-Szondian rules remain identifiable as such.

Initial evaluation states should remain minimal unless real corpus cases prove more structure is necessary: `SUPPORTED`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `UNRESOLVED`.

### Gate: `P2B_EXECUTABLE_INTERPRETATION_PASS`
PASS requires deterministic trigger evaluation for report-eligible interpretations, doctrine linkage, clinician approval and adversarial tests against unsupported certainty.

---

## P3 — CLINICAL EVIDENCE / GRAPH

### Purpose
Represent protocol facts, series facts, activated interpretations, contradictions and provenance in one traceable evidence structure.

### Gate: `P3_CLINICAL_GRAPH_PASS`
Every interpretive node must trace backward to executable claim -> doctrine entry -> source and forward to any report sentence that uses it.

---

## P4 — INTEGRATION

### Purpose
Integrate evidence without inventing reconciliation or increasing certainty.

Operations may include aggregation, deduplication, contradiction preservation, temporal integration, convergence and prioritization only under explicit rules.

### Gate: `P4_INTEGRATION_PASS`
Clinician-reviewed golden protocols demonstrate useful synthesis without source mutation, hidden inference or narrative smoothing of unresolved contradictions.

---

## P5 — DUAL REPORT ENGINE

### Clinician report
Preserves full Szondian terminology and doctrinal richness when evidence authorizes its use, including genetic, hereditary, genotropic, sexual, pathodiagnostic and transgenerational formulations.

### Client report
A separate downstream communication transformation: milder and phenomenological where appropriate, without modifying doctrine or clinician interpretation.

AI may formulate narrative only after deterministic planning and remains bounded by evidence, doctrine linkage and certainty ceilings.

### Gate: `P5_REPORT_PASS`
No unsupported clinical statement, no silent modernization in clinician output, no `UNRESOLVED` converted into positive assertion, and complete traceability for material statements.

---

## P6 — VALIDATION + RELEASE

### Purpose
Validate the complete system against canonical examples, adversarial cases and clinician-reviewed protocols.

Coverage includes administration, scoring, series, Doctrine Registry fidelity, executable interpretation, provenance, ambiguity preservation, integration, dual reporting, privacy/security and regression protection of the photograph-metadata boundary.

### Gate: `P6_RELEASE_PASS`
Release only after end-to-end reproducibility and clinician approval of both doctrinal fidelity and practical clinical usefulness.

---

## Restart protection rule

The reason for Szondi3 is structural separation between doctrine and executability. Future errors should be repaired in the layer where they occur. Another wholesale restart is not justified merely because an executable rule or integration strategy changes.

> **Never repair executable logic by rewriting the source doctrine it was supposed to represent.**
