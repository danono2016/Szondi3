## 2. The single cited work is now identified

OpenAlex records David & Rabinowitz 1951 as citing exactly **one** work, OpenAlex ID `W1967440059`.

Thus the only indexed cited work in David & Rabinowitz's 1951 instability-score paper is Deri's American Szondi exposition, not Böszörményi, Szondi's later quantitative chapter, or a Hungarian/German serial-fluctuation source.
