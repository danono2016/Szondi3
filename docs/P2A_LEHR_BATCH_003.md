## Scope

This batch covers `Kapitel II — Die Trieblehre der Schicksalsanalyse`, subsection `Ursprung der Triebe: die Gentheorie`, stopping immediately before `Die Triebkriterien der Schicksalsanalyse` at U000677.

## Doctrinal nucleus preserved

The batch records, source-near and without modernization:

- Szondi's explicit `Arbeitshypothese` that the sources of drives are genes;
- the assumed existence of specific `Triebgene` determining drive actions;
- intergenerational transmission of genes from parents to descendants;
- the claim that genes transmit tendencies from familial, ancestral/tribal and species past;
- the genetic explanation for the drive tendency to restore an earlier state;
- replacement of simple drive dualism by the dualism of `Triebgegensatzpaare` grounded in hereditary pairs/alleles;
- `Triebstrebung/Triebtendenz` as the genetically smallest drive unit;
- `Triebbedürfnis/Triebfaktor` as a paired hereditary unit;
- `Trieb/Triebvektor` as a synthesis/mixture of two needs rather than an original genobiological unit;
- factor and vector polarity;
- `Triebgegensatzpaar` as psychological expression of `Triebgenpaar`;
- the oral/sadistic `Probemischung` example and its distinction from `Sexualtrieb`;
- the preference for `Bedürfnispsychologie` over `Triebpsychologie` at the genobiological level;
- the role of internal/external factors, including the Ich, in the timing/process of need combination despite genetically prescribed laws.

## Epistemic-force control

The source does not present every genetic formulation with the same force. Entries tied to `Nach der Arbeitshypothese`, `wird angenommen`, or `müssen wir also annehmen` are explicitly marked so that P2A does not upgrade them into unqualified categorical fact. Other statements that Szondi presents categorically remain categorical.
