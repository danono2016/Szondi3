## Scope

This batch covers the complete `VORWORT` and establishes the source-local program of Ich-Analyse before the historical introduction begins.

## Core doctrinal objects

### Trieb-Analyse and Ich-Analyse within Triebpathologie

At U000469 Szondi presents the two analyses together as an attempt to represent the whole of `Triebpathologie`, conceived as a basis for psychopathology and clinical psychology.

### Partizipationsdrang as ursprüngliche Ich-Strebung

U000471 defines the original Ego striving through the urge to be one, equal, related and united with the other, the world, the All and oneself. Szondi states that this `ichhafte Partizipation` is still a `Triebstrebung` and bears the marks of a `Urtendenz`, while explicitly excluding a libidinal character.

The canonical token naming the tendency is OCR-corrupted. The admitted PDF, printed p. 17, was consulted only for this ambiguity and confirms `Partizipationsdrang`. The canonical `sourceExcerpt` remains literal and was not silently corrected.

### Development of the Ego from participation — epistemic qualification preserved

U000472 states that the Ego and its manifold functions develop from the Partizipationsdrang, while the functions themselves are no longer drive-like. Szondi qualifies this with `nach unserem Dafürhalten`; the registry therefore records the claim as `ASSUMPTION`, not as an unqualified fact.

### Pathodiagnostic thesis on disorders of Ego life

U000473 contains unusually strong wording: Szondi says `Wir konnten beweisen` that the most important disturbances of Ego life, including delusion formation in `Geisteskranke`, are fundamentally disturbances of the participation tendency. He then explicitly calls mentally ill persons fundamentally `triebkranke`, particularly `partizipationskranke`, people. These are retained as separate atomic entries at the source's categorical force and with historically sensitive/pathodiagnostic terminology intact.

### Integration program

U000476 states the ultimate aim of uniting/integrating contemporary depth-psychological directions under the primacy of Ich-Analyse. U000480 specifies the program as building an integrated, unified depth psychology from the `brauchbare Bausteine` of contemporary schools on the foundation of Ich-Analyse.

These are programmatic source-local statements. They do not authorize importing, reconciling or rewriting doctrines from other authors or Szondi volumes.
