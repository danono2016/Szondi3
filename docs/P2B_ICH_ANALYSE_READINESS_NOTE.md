## 1. IA-A core already source-verified

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_A_000040` — Egodiastole / Egosystole

Source-local doctrine states:

- Egodiastole = Ich-Ausdehnung and maps to p-Funktion;
- Egosystole = Ich-Einengung and maps to k-Funktion;
- this opposed functional activity underlies all Ich-Funktionen and determines all Ich-Stadien.

This is a better P2B foundation for p/k Ego semantics than inferring the polarity from isolated Lehrbuch labels.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_A_000043` — four elementary Ego functions and signs

PDF-arbitrated source-local mapping:

- `-p` = Projektion -> Einssein/Gleichsein/Partizipationsdrang;
- `+p` = Inflation -> Verdoppelung/Vollkommenheit/Allessein;
- `+k` = Introjektion -> Einverleibung/Inbesitznahme/Alleshaben;
- `-k` = Negation -> Verzichten/Verneinen/Verdrängen, with Desimagination/Destruktion as extreme end-direction in the source formulation.

The signs are PDF-arbitrated because canonical OCR corrupts `+p` and `+k`.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_A_000049` — Negation is broader than repression

This is a high-priority anti-inference candidate:

- Negation is a primordial Elementarfunktion / Ich-Radikal;
- Verdrängung is a **Unterform** of Negation, not the synonymous or superordinate category;
- the common testological marker of the Negation family is `-k`;
- concrete forms depend on p phase and quantity/intensity of negation.

Therefore a future rule `-k -> repression` would be source-distorting. At most, repression is one specifically conditioned form inside the broader Negation family.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_A_000050` — ordered developmental sequence

The four elementary functions are reused as developmental stages:

1. projektive Partizipation;
2. Inflation / Allessein;
3. Introjektion / Haben-Alleshaben;
4. Negation / Verzichten-Verneinen / adaptation.

This is an interpretive/developmental structure, not a license to infer chronological developmental level from one Sch reaction without a source-grounded discriminator.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_A_000051` — Integration / Desintegration

PDF arbitration establishes:

- `integriertes Ich = Sch ± ±`;
- `Desintegration = Sch 0 0`;
- source frames full Verschränkung of all four Ich-Radikale as an ideal and warns that any Ich-Unifunktion may become dangerous.

P2B caution:

- the testological image `Sch ±±` may be named according to the source as integrated Ego configuration;
- software must not silently upgrade the configuration into proof that an individual has achieved all existential, moral, spiritual, or developmental consequences attached to the source's idealized discussion.

## 2. IA-B creates mandatory methodological qualifications

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000006` — Vorder-Ich / Hinter-Ich definition

- Vorder-Ich combines the currently/constitutionally stronger elementary functions in the foreground;
- Hinter-Ich combines the weaker/background functions;
- together they constitute Globalität des Ichs / globales or integrated Ego;
- this is a functional complementarity, not two independent persons.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000007` — explicit retrospective correction

Szondi states that in `Experimentelle Triebdiagnostik` Ich-Analyse was built exclusively on Vorder-Ich and that, from the later dialectical Vorder-/Hinter-Ich perspective, the earlier analysis was **`eine halbierte`**.

This is directly relevant to integration governance:

> Sch/Ego P2B must not freeze the Lehrbuch/Vorder-Ich-only interpretive surface as if later Ich-Analyse had not explicitly qualified it.

This is a future `QUALIFIES/EXTENDS` relation candidate after both source-local objects are stable on `main`; no relation is committed here.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000008` — complementary Sch pairs

Theoretisches Komplementprofil organizes 16 Ich-Bilder into eight Vorder-/Hinter-Ich complement pairs, visually arbitrated from Table 9. Among them:

- `±± ↔ 00` = integrated/global Ego ↔ Desintegration/Ich-Verlust.

This reinforces that a visible Sch configuration belongs to a complementary Ego structure and should not be interpreted in isolation when the complementary profile is available/required.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000009` — complement versus actual integration

Source makes a critical distinction:

- theoretical possibility of Ergänzung is always present;
- actual Integration occurs **`nur sehr selten`**;
- complementary Ego existences may act successively or simultaneously.

P2B consequence for later review:

> Do not equate the theoretical complement relation, the testological label `integriertes Ich`, and demonstrated real-world achieved integration as if they were one evidential object.

The source's qualitative frequency phrase must not become a numeric threshold.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000010` — symptom analysis requires both Ego existences

Szondi's methodological wording is strong:

`Eine Symptomanalyse ... [ohne] Vorder-Ich ... und Hinter-Ich ... ist stets mangelhaft und oft irreführend.`

Source-local doctrine therefore requires character/symptom analysis to include Vorder-/Hinter-Ich dialectic and simultaneous/successive contrast.

This makes IA-B a **blocking dependency for mature Sch/Ego P2B**, not optional enrichment.

## 4. IA-B Batch 003 — Ich-Bild, Ich-Mechanismus and defense taxonomy

`P2A-IA-B-003` covers `U000680-U001064` and retains `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000015–000021`.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000015` — origin of defense versus site of defense

Abwehrmechanismen are unconscious Ich-Funktionen and defense activity originates from the Ego, but defense does **not** operate only inside the Sch vector. The Ego may protect itself through reactions in all four drive zones: Ich, Sexual, Affekt and Kontakt.

P2B consequence:

> `Sch` cannot be treated as the only possible location of Ego defense merely because defense originates from Ego.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000016` — `Ich-Bild` versus `Ich-Mechanismus`

This is a central integration discriminator:

- `Ich-Bild` = formal/descriptive, current/static Ego state;
- `Ich-Mechanismus` = functional/dynamic unconscious function or defense technique;
- the same Sch reaction can be read on these two distinct analytical levels.

P2B consequence:

> A Sch formula must not silently shift from static image label to dynamic defense mechanism. The level of interpretation must be explicit in the claim definition.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000017` — projection versus deprojection

Five projective defense forms are differentiated. Totale Projektion alone is projection as Ego unifunction; the four combined forms are grouped under `Deprojektion`, with Inflation, Introjektion, Zwang or Hemmung modifying the projective danger.

This blocks a future simplistic rule that every projective-looking Sch configuration is the same mechanism.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000018` — Inflation versus Deflation

Inflationsabwehr distinguishes totale Inflation from three forms of `Deflation`: Introinflation, Zwangsdeflation and Hemmung/negierte Inflation. The source contains an `Annahme` for part of the psychological mechanism, which must remain qualified.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000019` — introjective defense forms

Introjektive Abwehr is divided into totale Introjektion, inflaprojektive Introjektion and Intronegation/Zwangsmechanismus. These are not interchangeable labels for `+k`.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000020` — Negation / Verdrängung differentiation

The later detailed taxonomy states:

- totale Negation = Verdrängung;
- projektive Negation = Anpassung;
- Hemmung and Entfremdung also contain Negation but are classified under Deflation/Deprojektion according to dominant function;
- Verdrängung is distinguished by `0p`, `absolute Räumung`, and source-near `quasi eine Endstation des Verneinungsvorgangs`.

This complements the IA-A safeguard that Negation is broader than Verdrängung.

### `DR_SZ_IA_1956_B_000021` — defense and Sexualgefahr

IA-B maps several Sch defense configurations against types of sexual danger, but preserves qualifiers such as `scheinen` and `unter Umständen`. These are later, higher-risk interpretive mappings and should **not** be among the first executable Sch/Ego rules.
