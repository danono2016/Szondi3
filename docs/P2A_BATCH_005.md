## Scope

This source-order batch covers §6 `Die Heterosis` and stops immediately before §7. It preserves both the historical experimental-genetic scaffold and Szondi's specifically schicksalsanalytische human hypothesis without merging the two epistemic layers.

The historical layer includes the period definition of Heterosis, Johannsen's `heterozygote Konstruktion`, the Volldosis/Einzeldosis experimental paradigm, Gustafsson's summaries, selected plant/animal evidence, and the dose-dependent stimulation model. Attributed results remain attributed; they are not silently recast as original Szondian discoveries or as contemporary genetic validation.

The specifically Szondian layer begins at U000373-U000374. The registry preserves the exact qualifiers by which Szondi frames the transition: the experimental results are `heuristisch`, appear `in einer gewissen Richtung` to support a `kühne Annahme`, and the human proposition formulated in 1937 is explicitly called a `Hypothese`.

Within that hypothesis the source-near records preserve without modernization or sanitization:

- `krankmachende Gene` acting in `Einzeldosis` at the human level;
- `Konduktoren` as carriers of latent disease-producing genes;
- the transgenerational statement that carriers may be more `lebenstüchtig` precisely in the domain in which their `Vorfahren` were severely `erbkrank` and died;
- the example linking `krankmachende Gene der paranoiden Schizophrenie` in Einzeldosis with possible `Begabung zur Psychiatrie und Psychologie`;
- the categorical example linking `krankmachende Gene der Epilepsie` in kleiner Dosis with `homo sacer`-Berufe: `Mönch, Nonne, Richter, gerichtliche Medizin usw.`;
- the promised later source support in `Berufswahl und Schicksal`;
- the final conditional boundary: `Falls sich ... in Zukunft bestätigen`, under which Szondi only hopes that the basic thesis of the `Gentheorie des Schicksals` will be strengthened experimentally.

No P2B trigger, diagnostic activation rule, vocational-selection rule or runtime behavior is introduced.

## Assertion-strength discipline

The batch deliberately preserves local sentence force together with governing context. Thus U000375's epilepsy/vocation sentence remains categorical at the sentence level (`führen`), but its record is explicitly dependent on the U000374 `Hypothese`. Conversely, the schizophrenia example retains `können` as possibility. U000373 remains a tendency/heuristic qualifier rather than proof, and U000378 prevents retrospective inflation of the section into already-established experimental confirmation.

Two conclusions were recovered during the completion sweep rather than left as coverage-only detail: U000370 (`Letale Faktoren in Einzeldosis erhöhen die Modifikationsfähigkeit, die Plastizität`) and U000371 (`Die reine, homozygote Linie ist im Grunde eine Beeinträchtigung der Entwicklung`). They are stored as `DR_SZ_SA_1948_000173` and `000174`.
