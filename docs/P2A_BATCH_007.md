## Scope

This source-order batch covers `Zwei Erscheinungsformen der genischen Bestrebungen` and the complete section `Die fünf Hauptformen des Genotropismus`. It ends at U000433. U000434 begins `Die Gen-Quantitätstheorie und die multiple Allelie`.

The batch preserves the distinction between `genotypische Erscheinungsform` and `genotropische Erscheinungsform`, then registers all five principal forms of genotropism: Libido/Eroto-, Idealo-, Opero-, Morbo- and Thanatotropismus.

## Genotypische versus genotropische Erscheinungsform

U000412 defines the genotypic form as manifestation according to the inherited `Erbbild`. U000413 preserves Szondi's dosage distinction: dominant genes can manifest in `Einzeldosis`, recessive genes require `Doppeldosis`; the text states that `Erblehre` had until then recognized only the genotypic manifestation form.

U000414 defines the specifically schicksalsanalytische `genotropische Erscheinungsform`: a gene present in `Einzeldosis` in a heterozygous individual reaches manifestation indirectly by directing choices of love object, friend, ideal, business partner and professional connection toward persons who are themselves hidden or manifest carriers of the same gene.

U000415 gives Szondi's paradigmatic hereditary example: a hearing-intact descendant of deaf ancestors falls in love with another hearing-intact descendant of deaf ancestors.

## Libido/Eroto-Genotropismus

U000417 defines `Libido-Genotropismus` / `Eroto-Genotropismus` as the genotropic effect of latent genes in mate choice. U000419 gives the recessive-genetic mechanism by which Szondi treats this as a `genische Erscheinungsform`.

Szondi writes `Wir nehmen an` for the thesis that genotypic manifestation of a recessive gene in descendants is always preceded by genotropic action of latent genes in the conductor parents; the registry preserves this as `ASSUMPTION`. The following sentence that the genotropic form always precedes the genotypic form retains its stronger assertion force.

## Idealo-Genotropismus

U000420-U000421 define friend and ideal choice as another genotropic manifestation. `Idealo-Genotropismus` is represented source-near as the conductor choosing a genetically related (`genverwandt`) person as model or friend.

## Opero-Genotropismus

U000422-U000423 define `Opero-Genotropismus` as the manifestation of latent genes through occupational choice, by which the conductor forms ties to manifest homozygous carriers of the relevant gene.

The psychiatrist example crosses a page boundary and is preserved as one conceptual example through two canonical anchors, skipping page-header furniture U000424. Szondi describes a person with an openly mentally ill parent or sibling who remains apparently healthy, becomes a psychiatrist and spends most of life in an `Irrenhaus unter Geisteskranken`; Schicksalsanalyse interprets this as opero-genotropic manifestation of latent genes conditioning the `Anlage zu einer Geisteskrankheit`.

U000425 preserves without alteration Szondi's examples: `Feuerwehrmann` / `pyromanische Anlage`, `Kriminalist` / `kriminelle Anlage`, and `Friseur` / `homosexuelle Anlage`.

U000426 then gives Szondi's own explicit qualification: he does not claim that every firefighter is a latent pyromaniac, every criminalist a latent criminal, or every barber a latent homosexual. He says that such occupations can give the individual a `Möglichkeit` to live out the named `Triebansprüche` `in sozialer Weise`.

## Morbotropismus

U000427 defines the fourth form, `Krankheitswahl` / `Morbotropismus`. U000428 formulates the question why the same bodily damage can lead to different pathological consequences in different people.

U000429 gives Szondi's answer: the latent genes inherent in the individual determine the form of illness that appears as a reaction to external damage. The same unit states that in particular families varied physical and psychic traumas may repeatedly evoke the same bodily or mental illness.

## Thanatotropismus

U000430-U000431 introduce the fifth form: `Todeswahl`, `Thanatotropismus`.

U000432 states that the disease by which a person dies is not left to chance. U000433 continues the sentence across the page boundary and assigns determination of `Todesart` / `todbringende Krankheit` to `familiäre Gene` carried throughout life.

The final sentence uses `scheint`: suicide as cause of death, together with the means and method by which one brings about death by one's own will, appears to be the work of familial recessive genes. The registry preserves this weaker source force as `SUSPICION_INDICATION`.
