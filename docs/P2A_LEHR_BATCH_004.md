## Scope

This batch covers `Die Triebkriterien der Schicksalsanalyse`, including all five criteria and the genetic/pathopsychological reasoning immediately attached to them. It stops before `Das Triebsystem der Schicksalsanalyse` at U000708.

## Five criteria preserved

1. specific genetic determination (`spezifische Genbedingtheit`);
2. polarity of drive tendencies and needs;
3. drive tension / `Drang`;
4. physiological and pathopsychological criterion;
5. independent inheritance (`selbständiger Erbgang`).

## High-risk doctrine preserved source-near

The batch retains without modernization or sanitization:

- specific `Triebgene` and multiple allelic variations;
- the claim that one drive gene can manifest from an `inhumane Tat` to a `humane Tätigkeit`;
- inherited humane/inhumane `Schicksalsmöglichkeiten` together with the role Szondi assigns to personal responsibility, ideal formation and acquired identifications;
- the quantitative-not-qualitative hereditary distinction between psychiatric illness and health as an explicitly introduced hypothetical assumption;
- special status of `Ich-Triebgene` and the explicit reference to Ich-Analyse (1956) concerning `Partizipationstendenz` as `Urform des Ich-Triebs`;
- hallucination and delusion as defense mechanisms in Szondi's formulation;
- psychosis/neurosis as functional and reversible under an explicit `Arbeitshypothese`;
- latent preservation of a `gesunde Triebstruktur` during psychosis as Szondi's experimental claim;
- the four psychiatric/drive hereditary circles, including Szondi's sexual circle and its historical classifications;
- universal presence of corresponding drive needs in healthy individuals, including socialized/sublimated, professional/operotropic and genotropic-choice forms;
- the `Grundhypothese` of double-dose disease/death versus single-dose heterozygote vitality, including Szondi's explicit note that school geneticists rejected it;
- the weaker `scheint` formulation concerning double-dose paranoid schizophrenia versus single-dose aptitude for psychiatry;
- the explicit limitation that the four hereditary circles were not yet established with final `erbmathematische Genauigkeit`.
