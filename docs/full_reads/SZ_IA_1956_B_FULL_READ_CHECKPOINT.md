## High-confidence orientation map from the completed read

This is an orientation dossier only; all durable doctrine must still be atomized from exact canonical evidence.

1. **Dialectical Ich-Analyse.** The physical source begins directly with `ABSCHNITT III — DIALEKTISCHE ICH-ANALYSE`. Szondi distinguishes inner Ego dialectic from external Ego-drive/affect dialectic and organizes the inner dialectic around between-factor, within-factor and foreground/background oppositions.

2. **`Egodiastole` and `Egosystole`.** p is developed as Ego expansion and k as Ego restriction/position-taking. The source treats their complementary opposition as foundational for the unity of Ego life. Exact factor signs and reaction notation are OCR-sensitive and require local checking.

3. **Within-factor dialectic and historically sensitive sexual doctrine.** `Moll-Ich`, `Dur-Ich`, femininity/masculinity, bisexuality, `Zweigeschlechterwesen`, abandonment and compulsive formations are discussed through p/k dialectics. These claims must be preserved in their historical Szondian terminology without modernization or sanitization.

4. **`Vorder-Ich`, `Hinter-Ich` and complementary Ego fates.** Szondi defines foreground and background Ego formations, develops the `Komplementmethode`, groups 16 Ego existences into complementary Ego fates and distinguishes successive from simultaneous contrast effects. He extends the claimed action of the `Hinter-Ich` into wishes, partner choice, profession, character, symptoms, dreams and play.

5. **External defense doctrine.** The source distinguishes static `Ich-Bild` from dynamic `Ich-Mechanismus`, classifies defense mechanisms through Ego functions and through sexual, affective and contact reactions, and develops forms such as projection/deprojection, inflation/deflation, introjection/desintrojection and negation/denegation.

6. **Sublimation.** The source contrasts psychoanalytic and schicksalspsychological accounts and makes strong hereditary/allelic claims linking drive illness and corresponding forms of sublimation. The `Kain`–`Moses` construction and statements about allele series, dominance change, Ego and `Wahlschicksal` require especially careful assertion-strength preservation.

7. **Character and fate.** Character is treated as a portion of fate impressed into the Ego, with choice and introjection playing differentiated roles. Familial introjection, genealogical analysis, `Vordergänger`/`Hintergänger`, hereditary/environmental estimates and case material occur here. Statistical/twin-study statements must remain limited to the exact sample, method and degree of certainty stated by Szondi.

8. **`Diesseits` / `Jenseits` and the Ego as bridge-builder.** The later part returns to the Ego as `Pontifex oppositorum`, now in relation to transcendence beyond natural reality, temporal/futuristic function and the relation among reality, delirium, dream and faith.

9. **Wahn.** Delusion is classified functionally by the Ego function involved in pathological power distribution and unreal participation. Szondi presents empirical psychiatric material, percentages and transitions among projection-, inflation-, introjection- and negation/destruction-related delusional forms. Sample-bound empirical claims must not be universalized beyond the source.

10. **Dream.** Dream is developed as `autogene Partizipation`, with `Vordergänger`/`Hintergänger`, complementary Ego/drive fates, personal/familial/collective unconscious and the three source languages of symptom, choice/hereditary symptom and symbol. Szondi explicitly argues for an active Ego role in dream formation.

11. **Faith.** `Glaube` is treated as a special Ego function linked with participation, transcendence, spiritual power distribution and `Wahlschicksal`. The source also makes strong hereditary, psychopathological and therapeutic claims in this domain; these require exact source-local representation without contemporary correction or epistemic inflation.

12. **Final integration program.** The `Schlußwort` again presents Ich-Analyse as a possible integrating principle among depth-psychological schools through complementarity rather than amalgamation and returns to the `Pontifex oppositorum` construction.
