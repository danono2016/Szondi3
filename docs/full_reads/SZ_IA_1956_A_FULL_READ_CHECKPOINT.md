## High-confidence orientation map from the completed read

This section is a navigation map only. It does not replace atomic doctrine entries or their exact excerpts.

1. **Program of Ich-Analyse and integration of depth psychology.** The preface and introduction place *Ich-Analyse* together with *Trieb-Analyse* inside *Triebpathologie* and state the project of integrating usable components of contemporary depth-psychological schools under an Ich-analytic principle.

2. **Integration of the unconscious.** The first major part reviews the Freudian personal-repressed unconscious, the Jungian collective unconscious, and Szondi's `familiäres Unbewußtes`, together with the relation among symptom, symbol and choice (`Wahl`). The source repeatedly preserves the familial/hereditary level as literal Szondian doctrine rather than metaphor.

3. **Schicksalsanalytischer Ich-Begriff.** The work develops the Ego as `Pontifex oppositorum`, with functions of transcendence, integration and participation. Opposites and complementary pairs are treated as constitutive problems of Ego life rather than as material to be silently harmonized.

4. **`Partizipationsdrang`.** Participation is treated as a primordial Ego tendency toward `Einssein`, `Gleichsein`, relatedness and union. Szondi derives the later differentiated Ego functions from this primordial tendency while distinguishing the resulting Ego functions from the original drive-like tendency.

5. **Four elementary Ego functions.** The central source-local constructions are `Projektion`, `Inflation`, `Introjektion` and `Negation`, associated in the testological notation with the p/k factor tendencies. Their definitions, developmental relations, personal/collective/familial forms, healthy/pathological forms and limiting conditions require atomic extraction from their exact passages rather than a single summary entry.

6. **Projection and familial/genotropic doctrine.** The text differentiates personal, collective and familial projection. It explicitly connects `familiäre Projektion` with `Genotropismus`, `Ahnenbilder`, `familiäres Unbewußtes` and Wahl in love, friendship, profession, illness/symptom and death. These passages are high-risk hereditary/genotropic doctrine and require exact assertion-strength review.

7. **Inflation.** `Allessein`, `Ich-Verdoppelung`, bisexual/hermaphroditic and other historically sensitive sexual formulations occur as source doctrine. They must be preserved at source force and never normalized into contemporary terminology.

8. **Introjection.** `Alleshaben`, identification/incorporation, personal/collective/familial introjection, profession and familial fate appear as distinct constructions. The text links familial possibilities, Ego position-taking and character/fate in ways that require source-local atomic representation.

9. **Negation.** Negation is treated as an elementary Ego function rather than only an intellectual derivative. Repression, adaptation, inhibition, negativism, destruction and familial negation appear as differentiated forms or consequences. Familial negation is connected with resistance to an ancestral/familial fate and therefore with the possibility of `Wahlschicksal`.

10. **Integration of the Ego.** The end of this physical source develops integration of the elementary Ego radicals and the opposition between integration and disintegration. Tables summarizing projection, inflation, introjection and negation are layout-sensitive evidence zones and should be visually arbitrated where exact signs/columns matter.
