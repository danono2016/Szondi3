## Retained cores

- `000120` — two governing dimensions of a factor reaction: **quantity = strength of need; direction = Ego stance**.
- `000121` — compact **quantitative reaction taxonomy**: Null-, Durchschnitts- and Vollreaktion, including thresholds, Quantumspannung, ambivalent full reaction and the 28-reaction total.
- `000127` — compact **Nullreaktion doctrine**: current absence from foreground can reflect recent/indirect satisfaction or, rarely, constitutional weakness; null does **not** mean genetic absence; a specific null reaction does not by itself justify the corresponding manifest diagnosis.
- `000129` — **Partnerfaktor rule:** no factor should be interpreted in isolation from its partner-factor constellation.
- `000135` — the three general **Vollreaktion meanings**: dynamically strongest nonmanifest/wahllenkend-genotropic factor, premanifest tendency, or ambivalence.
- `000139` — dynamic sequence **Quantumspannung → Ambivalenz → Entladung**, observable only through serial profiles and not necessarily in every phase.
- `000145` — **Tendenzrichtung / Ego stance:** positive, negative, ambivalent; positive/negative Quantumspannung as hypertonic affirmation/negation.
- `000152` — EKP uses the same reaction protocol as VGP, with the source-specific handling of forced null retained for scoring/provenance.

## Fidelity safeguards

- Operational thresholds for Null/Durchschnitt/Voll, positive/negative/ambivalent reactions and Quantumspannung are not lost.
- The explicit rule that Nullreaktion does **not** mean absence from `Erbbestand/Genkonstitution` remains prominent.
- The anti-diagnostic example (`e` null ≠ manifest epilepsy) is folded into the governing anti-overinterpretation object rather than deleted conceptually.
- `Partnerfaktor` remains a standalone core because it directly constrains factor interpretation.
- Vollreaktion's wahllenkend/genotropic meaning remains separately retrievable because it can matter for later formula/root-factor interpretation.
- Serial dynamics remain distinct from static scoring definitions.
- No P2B executable trigger is created or modified by this compaction.
