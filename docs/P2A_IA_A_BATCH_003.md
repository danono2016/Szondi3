## Scope

This bounded source-order batch closes Szondi's initial classification of core/peripheral depth-psychological directions and extracts only the source-local Ich-Analyse doctrines needed for his own architecture: the criteria for a `Kernrichtung`, the two integration building blocks taken from sociological/cultural approaches, the egosystolic function, Transzendenz as an Ego function, and the hereditary/familial distinction between `Zwangsschicksal` and `Wahlschicksal`.

Historical summaries of Adler, Horney, Fromm, Sullivan, Husserl, Binswanger and Heidegger are retained in coverage as context. They are not promoted into primary doctrine of those authors and are not used to modify another source.

## Core doctrinal objects

### Kernrichtung criteria

U000520-U000525 give, explicitly `u. E.`, four criteria for a depth-psychological movement to count as a `Kernrichtung`: acceptance of the unconscious, discovery of a distinct functional domain within it, new diagnostic methods for its functions, and new healing methods for neuroses and psychoses. Szondi classifies Freud's original psychoanalysis, Jung's analytical psychology and his own Schicksalsanalyse as satisfying these criteria. The `u. E.` qualifier is preserved as `ASSUMPTION` rather than upgraded.

### Partizipationsstreben and Ich-Trieb

U000542 states that the person's need for power, acceptance and confirmation by the environment is represented in this book as `Partizipationsstreben des Ichs`, i.e. as `Ich-Trieb`.

### Stellungnehmendes Ich and Egosystole

U000543 represents cultural/social pressure and adaptation as the `stellungnehmende Ich`, the `egosystolische Ich-Funktion`. The same unit says this function corresponds, `unter anderem`, to Freud's reality principle and conditions the person's `Ich-Schicksal` through `Introjektionen` and `Negationen`. The phrase `unter anderem` is retained to avoid turning the comparison into an exhaustive identity.

### Transzendenz and Integration

U000559-U000560 apply the concept of transcendence to Szondi's own Ego definition. After PDF arbitration of a damaged canonical phrase, the printed text reads `Die transzendierende Instanz ist: das Ich.` Szondi then states directly: `Wir geben dem Ich die Funktion der «Transzendenz» und damit die Aufgabe der Integration.`

### Schicksal, Erbe and Wahl

U000564 says that Schicksalsanalyse was built, independently of Heidegger, on the connections among `Schicksal`, `Erbe` and `Wahl`. Szondi states that ancestors provide the building blocks of one's own fate and that each ancestor figures in the `familiäres Unbewußtes` as a particular `Schicksalsmöglichkeit`.

The same unit defines the familial unconscious as an inner fate plan containing many ancestors and therefore many, often polar-opposite, fate possibilities. Each ancestral figure has the `Tendenz` to function as a model for the descendant's fate; this is the `Zwang der Ahnen` in choices of love, friendship, profession, illness and death. The explicit word `Tendenz` is preserved as `TENDENCY`.

U000564 defines `Zwangsschicksal` as the part of fate conditioned by the ancestors and imposed on the person.

U000565 then defines the `Ich` as the instance that itself chooses among the familiarly given fate plans, rejects the others or integrates ancestral figures into a new figure. The part of fate freely chosen or integrated by the Ego is called `Wahlschicksal`.
