- `ösztönosztály`

but **does not list an inkonstancia method in the contents**.

### What this demonstrates

**A — SOURCE-VERIFIED negative at TOC level:** a major Hungarian university manual devoted to ten-profile Szondi work did not give the Inkonstanzmethode its own listed subsection in the contents. This makes it a lower-priority target for the missing `qu/t/c` cell than Noszlopi–Volosin 1972 or the primary *Szondiana I*.
