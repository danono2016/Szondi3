## Doctrinal content preserved in this batch

### Experimental Triebdiagnostik belongs inside Schicksalspsychologie

U000586 states that experimental drive diagnostics is only one research method of Schicksalspsychologie and that Schicksalspsychologie existed before the test. U000587 adds Szondi's retrospective methodological judgment that correct test interpretation depends on the specifically schicksalspsychological way of thinking and that purely technical test use is insufficient.

### Hereditary oppositions and individual fate

U000588 describes the person as born with hereditary oppositions in drive and Ego life and assigns Schicksalspsychologie the task of exposing the person's possible forms of existence so that an individual fate can unfold from them.

U000589-U000590 presents the broader fate model: ancestral claims / familial unconscious, hereditary drive nature as transformed through Ego defense, social environment, mental abilities and convictions, conscious Ego and Superego, and Geist all participate in shaping fate. Szondi explicitly contrasts favorable conditions that permit breaking a coercive inherited fate with unfavorable conditions that may repeat it.

### Profile, series and person must remain distinct

U000591 states that a person possesses multiple `Schicksalsmöglichkeiten`; a single `Triebprofil` reveals only one `Existenzmöglichkeit`. Szondi therefore requires a series of 8–10 profiles and says each profile must be interpreted as a whole. This passage is preserved as a source-level interpretive/methodological boundary, not collapsed into an executable runtime rule inside P2A.

### Existential rather than merely diagnostic use

U000592 says the test should primarily uncover possibilities of existence rather than simply fix psychiatric diagnoses; the current illness form is one fate form among possible forms. U000593 assigns the testologically trained clinician the task of promoting the existence possibility judged to offer the greatest chance to the individual and the community, rather than treating a current form as immutably fixed.

### Safeguards against interpretive error

U000594 says the edition adds systematic psychology of the eight factors and 64 vector images and states conditions for correct functioning of the test in order to reduce interpretive errors. U000595 says five interpretive methods are presented so that results can be checked against one another.
